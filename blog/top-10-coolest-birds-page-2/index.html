Top 10 Coolest Birds — Page 2

May 13, 2026
  • nature
  • birds

Here’s page 2 of the coolest birds around. Because 10 wasn’t enough.

10. Shoebill

Looks like a prehistoric relic and sounds like a machine gun. Enough said.

9. Secretary Bird

A long-legged raptor that stomps snakes to death. It’s a bird of prey that hunts on foot.

8. Atlantic Puffin

Clown of the sea. Cute beak, awkward walk, surprisingly good diver.

7. Sword-billed Hummingbird

Its beak is longer than its entire body. It’s the only bird that has to scratch itself with its foot.

6. Golden Pheasant

Looks like it was designed by a committee that couldn’t agree on a color palette. Gorgeous.

5. Andean Condor

Massive. Soars for hours without flapping. Basically a living glider.

4. Magnificent Frigatebird

Inflatable red throat pouch. Steals food from other birds. Not magnificent in character, but definitely in looks.

3. Great Horned Owl

The tiger of the sky. Silent flight, piercing stare, will eat pretty much anything.

2. Blue-footed Booby

Bright blue feet. Does a dance to impress mates. Peak evolutionary strategy.

1. Common Raven

Problem-solving genius. Plays tricks. Recognizes human faces. Basically a feathered hacker.

That’s the list.
